# Pricing Packages

General notes before the packages:

- **Prices are suggested starting ranges**, not fixed quotes — final pricing depends on content volume, integration complexity, and how much customization/branding is needed. Treat these as a starting point for a proposal, not a rate card to paste verbatim.
- **Timelines assume the client's content is ready to go** (documents, FAQs, brand assets). Timelines will stretch if content needs to be gathered or written from scratch — say so explicitly in any proposal rather than promising a date you can't control.
- All three packages build on the same proven architecture, so a client can reasonably upgrade from Basic → Standard → Premium later without a rebuild from zero.

---

## Basic — AI Chatbot / FAQ Assistant

**Suggested price range:** $600 – $1,500

**What it is:** A single AI agent answering from a defined FAQ/knowledge set, with a simple chat widget. No dashboard, no ticketing, no team management — just a working, grounded chatbot.

**Features**
- One AI agent, configured for your brand/tone
- Grounded answers from a provided FAQ set or a small set of documents
- Embeddable chat widget for a single website
- Fallback message when the AI doesn't know the answer
- Choice of AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or a zero-cost demo mode)

**Deliverables**
- Working chat widget deployed to your site
- Basic content ingestion for the FAQ/knowledge set you provide
- One round of tone/behavior adjustment

**Estimated timeline:** ~1–2 weeks after content is provided (varies with volume and revision rounds)

**Recommended target client:** A small business or solo founder that wants a simple, trustworthy FAQ bot on their site — not a full support operation yet.

---

## Standard — AI Chatbot + RAG + Knowledge Base + Dashboard

**Suggested price range:** $2,500 – $5,000

**What it is:** Everything in Basic, plus a real document/website ingestion pipeline, a management dashboard, and conversation history — the core of a genuine RAG-powered support agent a business can actually operate.

**Features**
- Everything in Basic
- Document upload (PDF/DOCX/TXT/CSV) and website ingestion into the knowledge base
- A dashboard: conversation history, knowledge base management, basic agent configuration
- Confidence scoring on AI answers
- Source attribution (which document an answer came from) visible to staff

**Deliverables**
- Everything in Basic
- Full ingestion pipeline for your content sources
- A working dashboard for managing knowledge and reviewing conversations
- Configuration walkthrough for your team

**Estimated timeline:** ~3–5 weeks after content is provided (varies with content volume and how much dashboard customization is requested)

**Recommended target client:** A growing business or SaaS company with an existing knowledge base and a real volume of repetitive support questions, ready to move past a simple FAQ bot.

---

## Premium — Full AI Customer Support Platform

**Suggested price range:** $6,000 – $15,000+

**What it is:** The complete system — everything in Standard, plus human handoff, ticketing, team roles, and an analytics dashboard. This is the "run your whole first-line support operation through it" tier.

**Features**
- Everything in Standard
- Confidence-based and keyword-based automatic human handoff
- Auto-generated support tickets linked to conversations, with priority/status management
- Team accounts with role-based permissions (Owner / Admin / Support Agent / Viewer)
- Analytics dashboard: resolution rate, escalation reasons, top question categories, response-time tracking
- In-app notifications for staff on escalation
- Custom branding across the widget and dashboard

**Deliverables**
- Everything in Standard
- Full human-handoff and ticketing workflow configured to your team's process
- Multi-user dashboard with roles set up for your team
- Analytics dashboard tailored to the metrics that matter to your business
- Deployment support and a training walkthrough for your support team

**Estimated timeline:** ~6–10 weeks after content and requirements are finalized, depending on scope, number of integrations requested, and revision rounds. *(A project of this scope genuinely takes multiple weeks of focused work — treat any promise of a "full platform in a few days" from any vendor, including this one, with skepticism.)*

**Recommended target client:** A business with a real support team, meaningful conversation volume, and a need to actually operationalize AI support — not just demo it. Also a strong fit for an agency that wants to white-label this for multiple end clients.

## Add-ons (quote separately, not included in any base package)

- Third-party integrations (Slack, Zendesk, HubSpot, etc.) — not part of the current SupportAI build; would be scoped and built per integration.
- Billing/subscription integration for a client who wants to resell seats.
- Custom outbound email delivery (transactional emails for notifications/password resets).
- Multi-page website crawling beyond a single URL per source.
- Migration/import from an existing helpdesk platform.
