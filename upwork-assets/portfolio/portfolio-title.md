# Portfolio Title Options

Use one of these as the Upwork portfolio project title. All are accurate to what was actually built (a working multi-tenant AI support platform, not a single chatbot script).

## Recommended

**SupportAI — AI Customer Support Platform with RAG-Powered Agents**

## Alternates

- SupportAI — Full-Stack AI Customer Support SaaS (RAG + Human Handoff)
- AI Support Agent Platform — Knowledge-Base RAG, Live Chat & Analytics Dashboard
- Multi-Tenant AI Helpdesk SaaS — Next.js, TypeScript, RAG, MongoDB
- SupportAI: An AI Agent That Answers From Your Own Docs, Escalates When It Should

## Notes for whoever posts this

- Lead with **"RAG-Powered"** and **"Human Handoff"** — these are the two things that separate this build from a basic GPT-wrapper chatbot, and they're both genuinely implemented (not just claimed).
- Avoid generic titles like "AI Chatbot App" — they undersell a project that includes multi-tenant auth, a full agent-configuration UI, ticketing, and an analytics dashboard.
